# Xwits

Xwits gives owner-run clinics and small businesses a dedicated AI department without the full-time payroll.

We automate sales, support, marketing and HR, inside the tools you already use. On this channel: how each system works, what AI is good and bad at, and how to tell whether your business needs it yet.

New systems start with a person approving their work. You own everything we build. Prices are public, in US dollars.



How we build: tenant isolation enforced in PostgreSQL, rules kept in the
schema, a person approving anything that cannot be undone, and every
integration published by its real state.
